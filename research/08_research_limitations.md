# Phase 2: Research Limitations

1. **Simulated Patent Searches:** The patent searches performed here rely on publicly accessible abstracts and academic summaries. A professional prior-art search by a registered patent attorney using proprietary databases (e.g., Derwent) is strictly required to verify freedom-to-operate.
2. **Access Limitations:** Some niche digital forensics papers (especially in closed IEEE or specialized military/intelligence journals) regarding advanced SQLite reconstruction were not fully accessible.
3. **Hardware & Scale:** We cannot currently predict the CPU/Memory limitations of the incremental recomputation engine on multi-gigabyte forensic datasets until the experimental phase is concluded.
4. **Legal Conclusions:** All statements regarding Section 3(k) compliance and patent eligibility are technical hypotheses, not legal advice.
